Tap a peg to hear the target note, or use the mic and tune until the needle sits in the green.

How to tune

Standard tuning, thickest string first: E2 · A2 · D3 · G3 · B3 · E4. Play one string at a time near the mic. If the needle points left the string is too low — tighten; right means too high — loosen. Always tune up to the note: if you overshoot, drop below and come back up — it holds tune better.

Open chords

Tap any chord to see it big and hear how it should sound. Numbers are fingers (1 = index … 4 = pinky). ○ = play open string, ✕ = don't play that string.

Practice with a pulse

Strumming in time matters more than perfect chords. Start slow — around 60–70 BPM — and only speed up once your chord changes land on the beat. The first beat of each bar is accented.
