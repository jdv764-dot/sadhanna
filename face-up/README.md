# Face Up · learn to sleep on your back

A 30-night trainer for stomach sleepers who want to become back sleepers.
Built as an installable web app (PWA), so it runs full-screen on an iPhone
like a native app — no App Store, no Mac, no developer account needed.

## What's inside

- **Tonight** — a nightly setup checklist (knee pillow, low head pillow,
  side barriers), a guided **4-7-8 breathing** timer you run while lying
  face-up, and a **morning check-in**: the position you fell asleep in,
  the position you woke up in, whether you started on your back, and a
  comfort rating.
- **Program** — a phased 30-night plan: *Set the stage* → *Start on your
  back* → *Return to base* → *Lock it in*. Your program night advances
  with every logged morning.
- **Progress** — nights logged, % of face-up wake-ups, current streak,
  a 14-night position chart, and an editable history.
- **Learn** — 9 short cards on the technique: the pillow rig, the reverse
  tennis-ball shirt trick, why you train the fall-asleep position first,
  the "return to base" rule, the week-one dip, and an honest safety note
  (snoring/sleep apnea and late pregnancy are reasons NOT to train supine).
- Everything is stored only on the device (localStorage); nothing is
  uploaded anywhere. Works offline after first load (service worker).

## Install on iPhone

1. Open the app's URL in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Launch it from the home screen — it opens full-screen like a native app.

## Deploy

The folder is static — deploy as-is to Vercel (or any static host).
